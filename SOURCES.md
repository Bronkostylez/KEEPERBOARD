# Sources read on 2026-10-07
All primary DFB football coaching sources. App text is original; suggested age bands, equipment quantities, durations and grouping rules are editorial adaptations, not official DFB standards.
- DFB Trainingspraxis: https://www.dfb-akademie.de/trainingspraxis/-/id-11011534 - small-sided games, transitions, 3v3 to 4v4, older youth examples.
- DFB Beste Trainingseinheit: https://www.dfb-akademie.de/beste-trainingseinheit/-/id-11011533 - activation, many touches, play and short waits.
- DFB Bambini North Pole: https://training-service.fussball.de/trainer/bambini/training-online/trainingseinheiten-detail/26012016-1117/ - broad movement games, simple playful ball tasks, no early specialisation.
- DFB F youth pass gates: https://www.dfb.de/fileadmin/_dfbdam/248792-500-f-au2-01.pdf - pair passing, gates and finishing.
- DFB goalkeeper basics: https://www.dfb-akademie.de/teil-4-grundtechniken-der-torhueterinnen/-/id-11011651 - catching, low balls, safe sequential fall mechanics.
- DFB technical/tactical goalkeeper training: https://training-service.fussball.de/trainer/d-juniorin/artikel/technisch-taktisches-torhuetertraining-beginnen-2298/ - position specific work from D youth, playful practice and outfield skills.
- DFB start of goalkeeper training: https://training-service.fussball.de/trainer/d-juniorin/artikel/der-anfang-der-torhueterausbildung-221/ - foundation and coordination.
